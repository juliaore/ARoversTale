ABOUT THE SHORT FILM

A story about the robots that discover Mars' mysteries.

A Rover's Tale is a short film made with hand-made illustrations, inspired by classic fairytales, to encourage school-aged teenagers to explore the world of space exploration. It was created for the NASA Space Apps 2026 Hackathon. We are the Biovisuales team, a design student and a biotechnology student.
